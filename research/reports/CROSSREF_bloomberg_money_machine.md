# Cross-reference: Bloomberg's "How Nvidia and OpenAI Fuel the AI Money Machine" against the register

Prepared 24 November 2026. The chart was supplied by Thomas as an image. It credits "Bloomberg News reporting" and has no date. Its market values (OpenAI $500 billion, Nvidia $4.5 trillion, Microsoft $3.9 trillion) and its deals (the $100 billion letter of intent, AMD's 6 gigawatt deal, the $300 billion Oracle contract) date it to about October 2025, thirteen months before this register.

Under the source policy one Bloomberg graphic is one tier 2 source. It can point to things to check, but it cannot put a number into the register. Every figure below is the register's, from filings and company releases.

## What the chart gets right

- Money and hardware run in both directions between Nvidia, OpenAI and the neoclouds. The register's eleven circular flows include all three of the chart's clusters: OpenAI, CoreWeave and Nscale.
- Microsoft sits upstream of the neoclouds as their customer. The register confirms it is the offtaker behind CoreWeave, Nebius, Nscale and IREN.

## What the chart misses, thirteen months on

1. **Guarantees.** The chart has investment, hardware, services and venture arrows, but none for credit support. Since it was drawn, Nvidia's reported guarantees rose to as much as $118 billion. $105 billion of that stands behind OpenAI's rent in Ohio, and $860 million behind Nscale's lease in Texas.
2. **Anthropic.** Not on the chart. Nvidia bought $10 billion of Anthropic shares in its November 2026 listing. Anthropic in turn owes up to $120.5 billion to SpaceX, $44.6 billion to Nscale and $41.3 billion to Lambda for Nvidia GPU capacity.
3. **SB Energy.** Not on the chart. Nvidia has $3 billion of equity in it, and its Ohio campus carries the $105 billion guaranty.
4. **The supply chain.** Prepayments and stakes in Amkor, KYEC, IBIDEN, Coherent, Lumentum, Marvell, Corning and Synopsys are all absent.
5. **The smaller neoclouds.** IREN, SharonAI and Firmus have Nvidia contracts or credit support; none is shown.

## What this changes in the register

Nothing in the register is contradicted. Two gaps are confirmed and added to the guarantee sweep:

- OpenAI's contract with Oracle: find the amount in an allowed source (an OpenAI or Oracle release or filing), and any guarantee behind Oracle's data center leases for it.
- AMD's share option to OpenAI: record it as a comparator, as Google's backstop of Fluidstack already is, because it shows a second chipmaker underwriting a lab's demand.

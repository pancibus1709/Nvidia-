# R3 report: NVIDIA announcements of capital, credit support or demand support (1 Jan 2023 to 23 Nov 2026)

**Date:** 23 November 2026. **Namespace:** r3 (evidence ev_30001 to ev_30092). **Sources:** nvidianews.nvidia.com, blogs.nvidia.com, nvidia.com, and NVIDIA 8-K filings with their Ex. 99.1 press releases and Ex. 99.2 CFO commentaries (all tier 1).

**Method.** Crawled the newsroom listing (`nvidianews.nvidia.com/news?page=1..220`, 2,199 items back to May 2022: 393 press releases, 1,806 blog posts) and screened every title and summary from 2023 onward. Fetched and read 85 releases and posts plus the NVIDIA 8-Ks (Items 1.01, 2.03, 8.01) and the quarterly earnings releases and CFO commentaries from Q4 FY23 to Q3 FY27. Ran site-restricted searches for seed counterparties. The listing crawl may not hold every release ever published (the newsroom shows about ten items per page); see Open questions.

**Reading rule.** Amounts are the release wording. "Headline" is the largest figure stated, never exposure. "Completed" means the release uses the past tense ("has invested", "invested", "has made an investment"). "Definitive" means a firm verb ("will invest", "is investing", "entered into") or a filed definitive agreement, even where the release does not name the agreement; the Conditions column says when a release is silent on this.

### Key facts (each with evidence)

- **SB Energy / OpenAI, PORTS-Pike (Ohio).** NVIDIA entered into residual value guaranties with SB Energy as lessor on leases of about 4.25 GW of IT load, with OpenAI as tenant. "NVIDIA's aggregate payment obligation is cumulatively capped at $105 billion for its initial commitment" (ev_30001, 8-K filed 2026-08-17). Guaranties take effect building by building at ready-for-service, first expected 2028 (ev_30005), across nine buildings, first in fiscal 2029 (ev_30027, CFO commentary 2026-11-17). Trigger: OpenAI insolvency default or payment failure (ev_30009). Term ends at the earliest of 20 years from lease commencement or OpenAI reaching a satisfactory credit rating, among others (ev_30006, ev_30007). OpenAI must reimburse and indemnify NVIDIA (ev_30008). Optional further credit support: about 3.8 GW in the 8-K (ev_30004) against 3.75 IT-GW in the press release (ev_30011). Max gross exposure shown as $105.0 billion at 25 Oct 2026 (ev_30024); no payments made to date (ev_30023). Separately NVIDIA "will invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy" (ev_30010).
- **OpenAI.** Letter of intent (ev_30018): "NVIDIA intends to invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI progressively" as each gigawatt is deployed (ev_30021, 2025-09-22). No NVIDIA release announces a definitive agreement or any funded tranche. An April 2026 NVIDIA blog restates OpenAI's 10 GW deployment commitment only (ev_30092).
- **Financing platforms.** MOUs with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to mobilize over $500 billion of third-party capital (ev_30016), "subject to execution of the final agreements" (ev_30015). NVIDIA "may provide a residual-value support mechanism for up to 25% of an opportunity" (ev_30013, blog 2026-08-11).
- **Equity stakes stated as completed:** Synopsys $2 billion at $414.79 (ev_30039, ev_30040); CoreWeave $2 billion at $87.20 (ev_30053, ev_30054); Marvell $2 billion (ev_30078); Thinking Machines Lab and SSI, amounts not disclosed (ev_30057, ev_30059); Point2, amount not disclosed (ev_30069); founding investor in Helix Digital Infrastructure and founding partner in the Brookfield Global AI Infrastructure Program, no amounts (ev_30017, ev_30074).

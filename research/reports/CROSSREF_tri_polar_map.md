# Cross-reference: "The Tri-Polar Map of AI Grand Strategy: Jensen Returns" against the register

Prepared 24 November 2026. The map was supplied by Thomas as an image; its source and date are not stated. The map is a strategy view. The register only records money, credit support and demand support that a filing, a company release or two named publications state. So the register can confirm or contradict the map's money claims. It cannot test the political claims ("White House put", "Congress pushback").

Figures are the register's confirmed figures (`research/reports/R9_system_view.md`): gross exposure at least $175.3 billion across 80 relationships.

## What the map leaves out that the money shows

1. **Guarantees are the main instrument.** Nvidia reported $118 billion of guarantee capacity at 25 October 2026, up from under $1 billion a year earlier. The map has no symbol for it.
2. **The smaller neoclouds carry the most layered support.** At Nscale Nvidia is shareholder, noteholder, lease guarantor and customer at once. IREN, SharonAI and Firmus have Nvidia contracts or credit support. None is on the map.
3. **A supply chain bank.** Nvidia prepays or invests in Amkor, KYEC, IBIDEN, SPIL, Unimicron, Coherent, Lumentum, Marvell, Corning and Synopsys. The map shows only TSM and INTC.
4. **Wall Street as co-lender.** Memorandums with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR aim to raise over $500 billion of outside money, with Nvidia offering residual value support on up to a quarter of a deal.
5. **Leases on Nvidia's own credit.** Nvidia had $74 billion of data center leases signed but not started, some of which it says it may pass to others.

## Bottom line

The map places Nvidia at the centre with a small "bank" box beside it. The register says the bank is the centre: 83% of the sized exposure sits with the two labs the map casts as Nvidia's rivals on open models. Its largest listed holding sits in the Musk pole. The map's three poles are better read as three groups of Nvidia borrowers.

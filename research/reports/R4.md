The only tier 2 outlet that fetched was Nikkei Asia.

So no figure in the register rests on tier 2 sources. Of 866 evidence records in `data/register/evidence.json`, 6 are tier 2, all Nikkei Asia (ev_110048 to ev_110052, ev_110063). They are used as dated market signals on Firmus (IPO withdrawal, A$7.9 billion offer size, 46 MW built of 912 MW contracted) and as a second report beside Firmus's own USD505 million release (tier 1, ev_110030). None of them is the only support for a displayed amount.

Under spec/04 a tier 2 figure displays only with two independent tier 2 sources. Several rows below have two outlets named in search results, but neither page was cached, so none qualifies yet.

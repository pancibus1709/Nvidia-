# R8 · The second ring: who else relies on Nvidia's relationships

Prepared 23 November 2026 from `depth2_links` in `data/register/relationships.json` (the cluster reports' R8 work, merged by the lead). Depth 2 only, per spec/02 R8. Amounts are display-eligible register amounts; "not disclosed" means the register holds no eligible figure. A depth 2 amount is the party's own contract, loan or commitment with the counterparty, not Nvidia exposure, and is never added to Nvidia's totals. Descriptions marked "analysis" in the source carry that label in the register. Where one party appears under several relationships with the same counterparty (for example Brookfield and Blackstone under each Crusoe or Firmus round) the row repeats because each relationship is a separate record.

## Five largest reliance chains (plain words, label: analysis)

1. **PORTS-Pike, Ohio.** SB Energy's data center backlog of about $430 billion is mostly rent from an OpenAI affiliate at PORTS-Pike (ev_120109). SB Energy says OpenAI is not an investment-grade tenant and that the campus's financeability depends in significant part on Nvidia's residual value guaranty, capped at $105 billion (ev_100024, ev_30001; `rel_openai_gu_2026`). SB Energy targets up to 90% debt per building (ev_100019), but no project debt exists yet (ev_100020). So the future PORTS-Pike lenders, SB Energy's shareholders (SoftBank controlling, ev_120110) and its planned $3.0 billion Citibank revolver (ev_40105) all lean on Nvidia's credit, not OpenAI's.
2. **Anthropic to SpaceX to its GPU lessors.** Anthropic, in which Nvidia bought $10.0 billion of stock (ev_120038; `rel_anthropic_eq_2026`), has agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion on SpaceXAI's Nvidia GPU capacity, cancellable on 90 days notice after initial periods (ev_120047, ev_120048). SpaceX finances those GPUs through Valor leases recorded as $12.9 billion of debt (ev_120085) and an Apollo-managed lessor commitment of about $40 billion (ev_120094) (`rel_spacex_eq_2026`). The lessors rely on SpaceX revenue, much of which is Anthropic, which is partly funded by Nvidia; Nvidia also holds SpaceX shares.
3. **Nscale.** Microsoft (up to $43.8 billion, ev_40027) and Anthropic (up to $44.6 billion, ev_40029) are Nscale's offtakers, and Nscale has no binding financing for the Anthropic contract (ev_40030). JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs lend $1.85 billion against GPUs at Ward County (ev_40035), the building whose lease Nvidia guarantees up to $860.34 million (`rel_nscale_gu_2025`, ev_40003); Nvidia's $2.0 billion of unsecured notes sit below them (`rel_nscale_fc_2026`). A Nscale failure would hit Nvidia's guarantee, notes and preferred shares, and two large customers' capacity plans, at once.
4. **Nebius.** Meta (up to about $27 billion, including up to $15 billion to buy capacity Nebius does not sell, ev_40079) and Microsoft (up to about $17.4 billion, ev_40084) are the offtakers; MUFG ($751.8 million term loan, ev_40064) and Citi ($3.5 billion, 364 days, ev_40072) lend after Nvidia's $2.0 billion warrant (`rel_nebius_eq_2026`). Here the backstop comes from Meta, not Nvidia.
5. **CoreWeave.** Meta (up to about $21.0 billion, ev_20103) and OpenAI (up to about $11.9 billion, ev_20199) buy reserved capacity ahead of Nvidia's $6.3 billion residual order (`rel_coreweave_bk_2025`). Lenders (JPMorgan on DDTL 5.5, $1,555 million; MUFG on DDTL 3.0, 4.0 and 5.5) and holders of the $4.2 billion 2033 convertible notes rank ahead of Nvidia's common stock (`rel_coreweave_eq_2025`). No credit agreement read names Nvidia, and the order ends after a 90 day insolvency (ev_20006), so lenders cannot count on it.

Other chains in the table: Brookfield's up to $9 billion NAVER vehicle relies on NAVER's eight-year fee guarantee, beside Nvidia's $1 billion NAVER commitment (`rel_naver_eq_2026`); Blackstone's $10 billion Firmus facility sits beside Nvidia's Firmus equity and Batam credit support (`rel_firmus_bk_2026`); Lambda's term loan ($926 million) and fixed rate facility ($1.008 billion) rely on investment-grade offtakers, one of which Bloomberg reports is tied to Nvidia (unconfirmed, R4 row 25).
